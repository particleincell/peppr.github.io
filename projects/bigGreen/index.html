Bell Jar Chamber Rebuild

Rebuilding a 30-inch bell-jar chamber to expand PEPPR's test capability for plasma and propulsion research.

← All projectsContact the team
Rationale

Bringing space enviornment up to pace with abitious EP experiments

A dependable vacuum facility is the uncontested foundation for all of PEPPR's experimental work. This rebuild modernizes an existing 30-inch bell jar around a new well and supporting vacuum hardware, paving a clearer path to various plasma and thruster tests.

Students contribute to all aspects of the facility-development process: integration, maintenance, operating procedures, and instrumentation.

Goals

The purpose of rebuilding

  • Reduce points of complication by omitting vestigial plumbing and out dated hardware.
  • Streamline chamber operation with ample electrical and fluid pass throughs for full envelope of plasma testing.
  • Establish safe, repeatable operating procedures.
Progress

Current work and next steps

The team is rebuilding the chamber around its new well and planning the pump, valve, and control integration. Upon arrival of the new well the team will begin diassembling vacuum plumbing, pneumatics, and electronics for installation. The reassembly of these components give the opportunity to slim down the system, reduce unecessary complexity, and develop a hands on understanding of chamber operation.
