Research project · Active

HET-01

Cal Poly's first custom-designed Hall-effect thruster: a sub-kilowatt system driven by electromagnets and krypton.

← All projectsContact the team
Rationale

Bringing HET technology to Cal Poly

HET-01 gives PEPPR members an opportunity to develop a practical skills from sizing and magnetic-circuit design to fabrication, integration, and testing. The project is scoped to be an introduction to EP thruster design and serve as the plasma source for our teams working on plasma diagnostic projects.

The team is developing a resilient sub-kilowatt thruster in the first of a series that will fascilitate future testing and iteration.

Goals

What the team is working toward

  • Manufacture and assemble thruster body, channel, electromagnets and integration structure.
  • Validate magnetic-field strength is as designed.
  • Test and characterize hollow cathode.
  • Characterize performance in STELLA vacuum chamber.
Progress

Current work and next steps

The project has completed preliminary scaling, channel-geometry, and material-compatibility work. Current effort is focused on magnet testing, followed by manufacturing and thrust stand integration to begin testing.
