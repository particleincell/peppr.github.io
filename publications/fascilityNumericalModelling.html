Numerical Investigation of Plasma Dynamics in Cal Poly’s Electrical Propulsion Vacuum Facilities

Developing numerical plasma simulations to predict electric-propulsion plume behavior and facility effects, with experimental probe measurements planned to validate the models.

Citation

D. Tennant and A. Presto, “Numerical Investigation of Plasma Dynamics in Cal Poly’s Electrical Propulsion Vacuum Facilities,” 2026 Regional Student Conferences, 2026. doi:10.2514/6.2026-112087.

Abstract

This paper presents current effort to develop numerical simulation capability to complement Cal Poly Aero Department's research into electric propulsion (EP). The primary effort utilizes development of CAD models of the vacuum facilities along with models for a gridded ion thruster and a Hall thruster, generating surface meshes and subsequently utilizing existing plasma thruster modeling code to produce predictions of plume densities, plasma potentials, and wall current fluxes across a set of different operating conditions. Future work will obtain representative experimental probe measurements to validate the simulations.
