PEPPR: Poly Electric Propulsion and Plasma Research

About

PEPPR is a Cal Poly student-led "Independent Research Association" (IRA) organized under the Aerospace Engineering department. We are currently building a low power Hall Effect Thruster for use in air-breathing applications, while also investigating other electric propulsion (EP) technologies such as gridded ion thrusters, hollow cathods, and RF-driven "Pocket Rocket".
